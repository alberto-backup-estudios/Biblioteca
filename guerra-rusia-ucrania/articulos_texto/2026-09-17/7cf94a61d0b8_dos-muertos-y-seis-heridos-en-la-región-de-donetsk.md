## Noticia Completa Traducida al Español:

Los ocupantes rusos bombardearon asentamientos en la región de Donetsk 23 veces durante el último día, lo que provocó dos muertos y seis heridos, informó el jefe de la Administración Militar Regional, Vadym Filashkin.

"Distrito de Kramatorsk. Una persona murió en Maiaky, en la comunidad de Sviatohirsk. En Karpivka, en la comunidad de Mykolaivka, ocho casas privadas sufrieron daños. En Sloviansk, dos personas resultaron heridas y tres casas privadas, una oficina de correos, un restaurante, siete coches y una gasolinera sufrieron daños", escribió en Telegram el jueves por la mañana.

Según la administración militar regional, en Kramatorsk un coche resultó dañado; una persona murió y otra resultó herida en Malotaranivka; y dos personas resultaron heridas en Bilenke.

En Shostakivka, en la comunidad de Novodonetske, resultaron dañados una planta de producción y tres almacenes, mientras que otros dos almacenes sufrieron daños en Samiilivka. En Petrivka Persha, dentro de la comunidad de Oleksandrivka, resultaron dañados una casa privada, tres dependencias y un automóvil, y en Ocheretyne una casa privada.

"En total, durante el día, los rusos bombardearon los asentamientos en la región de Donetsk 23 veces. Del frente fueron evacuadas 718 personas, entre ellas 74 niños", informó el jefe de la Administración Militar Regional.

## Texto Original (EN):

### Two killed, 6 injured in Donetsk region in past day

Russian occupiers shelled settlements in the Donetsk region 23 times over the past day, resulting in two deaths and six injuries, Regional Military Administration Head Vadym Filashkin has reported.

"Kramatorsk district. one person died in Maiaky within Sviatohirsk community. In Karpivka within Mykolaivka community, eight private houses were damaged. In Sloviansk, two people were injured, and three private houses, a post office, a restaurant, seven cars, and a gas station were damaged," he wrote on Telegram on Thursday morning.

According to Regional Military Administration, a car was damaged in Kramatorsk; one person was killed and one injured in Malotaranivka; and two people were injured in Bilenke.

In Shostakivka within Novodonetske community, a production facility and three warehouses were damaged, while another two warehouses were damaged in Samiilivka. In Petrivka Persha within the Oleksandrivka community, a private house, three outbuildings, and a car were damaged, and a private house was damaged in Ocheretyne.

"In total, over the day, the Russians shelled settlements in Donetsk region 23 times. From the frontline, 718 people were evacuated, including 74 children," Regional Military Administration head reported.
