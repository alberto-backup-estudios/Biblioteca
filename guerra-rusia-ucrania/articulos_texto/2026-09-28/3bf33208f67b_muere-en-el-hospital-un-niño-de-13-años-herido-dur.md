## Noticia Completa Traducida al Español:

Oleksandr Tsiatska, de trece años, que sufrió graves quemaduras como resultado de un ataque ruso contra la aldea de Katerynivka en el óblast de Járkov el 21 de septiembre, ha muerto. Su madre murió en el mismo ataque.

Fuente: Serhii Zelenskyi, jefe del Lozova 
montón

Cita: "Oleksandr Tsiatska ha muerto. Lozova hromada vuelve a inclinar la cabeza con profundo dolor. Las palabras no pueden expresar la desesperación y el dolor que llenan los corazones de cada uno de nosotros en este momento".

Detalles: Según Zelenskyi, los médicos intentaron salvar la vida de Oleksandr, pero murió a causa de las heridas.

Oleksandr sufrió quemaduras en aproximadamente el 60% de su cuerpo, de las cuales el 20% fueron de gravedad crítica.

## Texto Original (EN):

### Boy aged 13 injured during Russian strike on Kharkiv Oblast dies in hospital

Thirteen-year-old Oleksandr Tsiatska, who sustained severe burns as a result of a Russian strike on the village of Katerynivka in Kharkiv Oblast on 21 September, has died. His mother was killed in the same attack.

Source: Serhii Zelenskyi, Head of the Lozova 
hromada

, on Telegram

Quote: "Oleksandr Tsiatska has died. The Lozova hromada bows its head in deep sorrow once again. Words cannot express the despair and grief that fill the hearts of every one of us right now."

Details: According to Zelenskyi, doctors tried to save Oleksandr's life, but he died from his injuries.

Oleksandr suffered burns to approximately 60% of his body, with 20% being critically severe.

Background: On 21 September, Russian forces struck a house in the village of Katerynivka in the Lozova hromada in Kharkiv Oblast, using a drone. Oleksandr's mother, Iryna Tsiatska, was killed during the attack. Thirteen-year-old Oleksandr was hospitalised with severe burns. According to Zelenskyi, Iryna was a master craftswoman and volunteer who assisted the Ukrainian military.
