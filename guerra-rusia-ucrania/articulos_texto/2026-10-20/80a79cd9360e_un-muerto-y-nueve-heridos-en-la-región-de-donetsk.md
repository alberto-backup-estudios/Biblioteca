## Noticia Completa Traducida al Español:

Las fuerzas rusas bombardearon asentamientos en la región de Donetsk 30 veces en las últimas 24 horas, dejando un muerto y nueve heridos, informó el jefe de la Administración Militar Regional, Vadym Filashkin.

Además, 296 personas, entre ellas 43 niños, fueron evacuadas de la zona del frente.

"Distrito de Kramatorsk. En Rayhorodok de Mykolayivka hromada, una persona resultó herida y un coche y una motocicleta resultaron dañados. En Slaviansk, una persona murió y tres resultaron heridas; dos edificios de apartamentos, 41 casas privadas, cuatro coches y un garaje resultaron dañados. En Kramatorsk, tres personas resultaron heridas", escribió Filashkin en Telegram.

En Ocheretyne de Oleksandrivka hromada una persona resultó herida; en Dmytro-Dariyivka, una persona resultó herida y un camión resultó dañado; en Znamenshytsia (Znamenivka), una casa resultó dañada; En Starovarvarivka resultaron dañados cinco casas particulares, dos edificios administrativos y una iglesia, añadió el jefe de la administración militar regional.

## Texto Original (EN):

### One killed, 9 injured in Donetsk region in past 24 hours – official

Russian forces shelled settlements across Donetsk region 30 times over the past 24 hours, leaving one person dead and nine others injured, Head of the Regional Military Administration Vadym Filashkin reported.

In addition, 296 people, including 43 children, were evacuated from the frontline area.

"Kramatorsk district. In Rayhorodok of Mykolayivka hromada, one person was injured, and a car and a moped were damaged. In Sloviansk, one person was killed and three were injured; two apartment buildings, 41 private houses, four cars, and a garage were damaged. In Kramatorsk, three people were injured," Filashkin wrote on Telegram.

In Ocheretyne of Oleksandrivka hromada, one person was injured; in Dmytro-Dariyivka, one person was injured and a truck was damaged; in Znamenshytsia (Znamenivka), a house was damaged; in Starovarvarivka, five private houses, two administrative buildings, and a church were damaged, the head of the Regional Military Administration added.
