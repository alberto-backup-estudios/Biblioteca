The Group 10 unit of the Active Action Department of Defence Intelligence of Ukraine has captured 29 Russians during a raid in Sumy Oblast.

Source: Active Action Department of Defence Intelligence of Ukraine

Details: The department said that, in addition to taking prisoners, it had killed 108 Russian soldiers and wounded 157 others.

The operation lasted around seven weeks and was carried out in two stages. A total of 40 special forces personnel took part directly in the active phase of the operation.

The Active Action Department cleared more than 600 hectares of forest of Russian troops and handed the positions over to Ukrainian defence forces.
