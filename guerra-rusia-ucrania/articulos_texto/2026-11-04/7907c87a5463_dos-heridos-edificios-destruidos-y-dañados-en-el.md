## Noticia Completa Traducida al Español:

Dos personas resultaron heridas en un ataque ruso en la provincia de Odesa. Un almacén que almacenaba calzado y dos automóviles quedaron destruidos y un depósito postal resultó dañado.

Fuente: Servicio Estatal de Emergencia (SES) de Ucrania

Cita de SES: "Óblast de Odesa: dos personas resultaron heridas a consecuencia del ataque ruso. Un almacén donde se almacenaba calzado resultó dañado, se produjo un incendio y dos coches quedaron destruidos.

En otro lugar, un depósito postal resultó dañado a causa de una huelga y un camión se incendió".

Detalles: Más de 60 bomberos fueron desplegados para hacer frente a las secuelas del ataque ruso. Trabajaron bajo la amenaza de nuevas huelgas.

A las 15:20 todos los incendios habían sido extinguidos.

## Texto Original (EN):

### Two injured, buildings destroyed and damaged in Russian attack on Odesa Oblast – photos, video

Two people have been injured in a Russian attack in Odesa Oblast. A warehouse storing footwear and two cars have been destroyed, and a postal depot has been damaged.

Source: State Emergency Service (SES) of Ukraine

Quote from SES: "Odesa Oblast: two people have been injured as a result of a Russian attack. A warehouse storing footwear has been damaged, a fire broke out and two cars have been destroyed.

At another location, a postal depot has been damaged as a result of a strike and a lorry caught fire."

Details: More than 60 firefighters were deployed to deal with the aftermath of the Russian attack. They worked under the threat of further strikes.

As of 15:20, all fires had been extinguished.
