## Noticia Completa Traducida al Español:

Ucrania todavía está registrando problemas con la red Starlink, dicen fuentes dentro del Ministerio de Defensa, después de que Donald Trump dijera que Elon Musk había revertido los límites en el enlace ascendente del satélite.

Las fuentes dijeron a la BBC que se estaban registrando casos de drones que experimentaban pérdidas de conexión a velocidades de 161 km/h (100 mph), lo que obstaculizaba las operaciones militares.

El presidente de Estados Unidos había negado haber dado instrucciones al multimillonario tecnológico para restringir la conectividad a la contraparte de grado militar de Starlink, Starshield, antes de decir el jueves que Musk lo había "devuelto".

Musk dijo anteriormente que no estaba detrás de la desaceleración, que afecta la capacidad de Ucrania para utilizar drones de ataque y coordinar operaciones defensivas.

Las fuentes del Ministerio de Defensa de Ucrania dijeron que el ejército estaba monitoreando constantemente el desempeño de las redes Starlink y Starshield.

Agregaron que "actualmente no hay confirmación de que estas restricciones hayan sido levantadas".

## Texto Original (EN):

### Ukraine says Starlink still restricted after Trump says limits lifted

Ukraine is still recording issues with the Starlink network, sources within the defence ministry say, after Donald Trump said limits had been reversed by Elon Musk who owns the satellite system.

The sources told the BBC that instances of drones experiencing connection loss at speeds of 161km/h (100mph) were being recorded, hampering military operations.

The US president had denied instructing the tech billionaire to restrict connectivity to Starlink's military-grade counterpart, Starshield, before saying on Thursday that Musk had "put it back".

Asked on Friday, Trump said: "If there is a problem, I will get it straightened out."

Trump added: "I have not spoken to him. I might have to, but I have not. I've heard it was fixed, but if it's not, I'll speak to him."

Musk previously said he was not behind the slowdown, which affected Ukraine's ability to use attack drones and co-ordinate defensive operations.
