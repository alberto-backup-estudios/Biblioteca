## Noticia Completa Traducida al Español:

Las fuerzas de ocupación rusas lanzaron más de 40 ataques contra cinco distritos de la región de Dnipropetrovsk utilizando drones, artillería y bombas aéreas, informó Oleksandr Hanzha, jefe de la Administración Militar Regional de Dnipropetrovsk.

"Tres personas murieron. Cinco resultaron heridas", escribió Hanzha en su canal de Telegram.

En el distrito de Nikopol fueron atacadas la ciudad de Nikopol y las comunidades de Marhanets, Pokrovske, Chervonohryhorivka y Mozolivka. Una casa particular, camiones e infraestructura resultaron dañados.

"Cuatro personas resultaron heridas. Hombres de 58, 28 y 41 años fueron hospitalizados en estado moderado. Un hombre de 45 años recibirá tratamiento ambulatorio", dijo Hanzha.

En el distrito de Kamyaanske, las comunidades de Bozhedarivka, Verkhivtseve y Piatykhatky resultaron afectadas. Se produjeron incendios. Tres personas murieron. Un hombre de 60 años fue trasladado al hospital en estado moderado.

En la zona de Kryvyi Rih el enemigo atacó la comunidad de Sofiyivka. La infraestructura resultó dañada.

## Texto Original (EN):

### Three killed, five injured in Russian shelling in Dnipropetrovsk region – Regional Military Administration

Russian occupation forces launched over 40 attacks on five districts of Dnipropetrovsk region using drones, artillery, and aerial bombs, reported Oleksandr Hanzha, head of Dnipropetrovsk Regional Military Administration.

"Three people were killed. Five were injured," Hanzha wrote on his Telegram channel.

In Nikopol district, the city of Nikopol and Marhanets, Pokrovske, Chervonohryhorivka, and Mozolivka communities came under fire. A private house, trucks, and infrastructure were damaged.

"Four people were injured. Men aged 58, 28, and 41 were hospitalized in moderate condition. A 45-year-old man will receive outpatient treatment," Hanzha said.

In Kamyaanske district, Bozhedarivka, Verkhivtseve, and Piatykhatky communities were affected. Fires broke out. Three people were killed. A 60-year-old man was taken to the hospital in moderate condition.

In Kryvyi Rih area, the enemy struck Sofiyivka community. Infrastructure was damaged.
