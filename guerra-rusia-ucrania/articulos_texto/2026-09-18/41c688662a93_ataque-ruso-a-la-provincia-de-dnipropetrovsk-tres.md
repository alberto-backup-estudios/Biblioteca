## Texto Original (EN):

### Russian attack on Dnipropetrovsk Oblast: three killed, five injured – photos

Russian forces attacked five districts of Dnipropetrovsk Oblast over 40 times using drones, artillery, and an aerial bomb on 17 September.

Source: Oleksandr Hanzha, Head of Dnipropetrovsk Oblast Military Administration, on Telegram

Quote: "The city of Nikopol and Marhanets, Pokrovsk, Chervonohryhorivka and Mozolevske 
hromadas

 came under attack in the Nikopol district. A house, lorries and infrastructure have been damaged.

Four people have been injured. Men aged 58, 28 and 41 were hospitalised in a moderate condition. A 45-year-old man will receive outpatient treatment."

Details: Infrastructure was also damaged and three people were killed in the Kamianskyi district. A 60-year-old man was taken to hospital in a moderate condition.

The Russians also hit the Kryvyi Rih, Pavlohrad and Synelnykove districts.
