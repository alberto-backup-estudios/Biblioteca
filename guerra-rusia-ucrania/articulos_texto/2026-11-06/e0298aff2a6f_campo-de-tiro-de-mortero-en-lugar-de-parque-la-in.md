### Mortar firing range instead of park: Ukraine's Defence Intelligence reveals how Russians are destroying Ukrainian nature reserves

Russian forces have established a mortar firing range in Pryazovia National Nature Park and destroyed bird nesting sites in Meotyda National Nature Park. Their actions have caused billions of dollars in environmental damage and bear the hallmarks of war crimes.

Source: Defence Intelligence of Ukraine (DIU)

Details: Data from the State Environmental Inspectorate of the Southern District indicate that damage to protected natural areas in Kherson Oblast alone had been estimated at more than UAH 1.2 trillion (approximately US$28 billion) as of October 2026.

Russian forces are devastating Ukrainian ecosystems and protected areas, turning them into battlefields, military fortifications and heavily polluted sites, DIU noted.

Quote: "The aggressor state, Russia, is establishing so-called 'environmental protection institutions' in the temporarily occupied territories of Donetsk, Luhansk and Kherson oblasts. The functioning of these sham bodies is aimed at strengthening occupation control over the seized hectares of protected land and Ukraine's natural resources."
