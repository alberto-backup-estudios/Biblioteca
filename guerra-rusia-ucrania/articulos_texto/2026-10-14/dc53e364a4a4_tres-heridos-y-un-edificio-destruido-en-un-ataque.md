## Texto Original (EN):

### Three injured and building destroyed in Russian drone attack on Zaporizhzhia – photos

Three people were injured in a Russian drone strike on Zaporizhzhia on 14 October.

Source: State Emergency Service of Ukraine (SES)

Quote from SES: "Three people have been injured as a result of a Russian UAV attack. A house has been damaged at one address. Firefighters extinguished the fire. A two-storey residential building has been destroyed at another address."

Details: Psychologists from the State Emergency Service of Ukraine provided assistance to seven people.

Search and rescue operations have been completed.
