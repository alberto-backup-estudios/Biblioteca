Más de tres docenas de ellos estaban sentados acurrucados contra una pared, con máscaras faciales debido a la pandemia de Covid. Un periodista ruso los describió como "pálidos y con falta de sueño". Al entrar, Putin se sentó en el lado opuesto del salón, creando un gran espacio entre él y los empresarios. Como de costumbre, no llevaba máscara.

"Me gustaría pedirles que muestren comprensión por lo que está pasando", dijo, "y que trabajen junto con el gobierno para encontrar las herramientas que apoyen la producción, la economía y el empleo".

En el vídeo de la reunión publicado por el Kremlin, ninguno de los multimillonarios invitados dijo una palabra.

## Texto Original (EN):

### Russia's mega-rich are already imagining a future after Putin

Hours after launching his full-scale invasion of Ukraine on 24 February 2022, Vladimir Putin gathered Russia's richest men in the Kremlin's cavernous Yekaterininsky Hall.

More than three dozen of them sat huddled along a wall, wearing face masks because of the Covid pandemic. One Russian newspaper reporter described them as "pale and sleep-deprived". On entering, Putin sat down at the opposite side of the hall, creating a vast space between him and the businessmen. As usual, he was wearing no mask.

Using his typically coded language, he said he expected them to support the war.

"I would like to call on you to show understanding of what is going on," he said, "and work together with the government to find the tools which would support production, the economy, jobs."

In the Kremlin-published video of the meeting, none of the invited billionaires said a word.

That silence has become a feature of the Russian super-rich. Over the decades of Putin's rule, they have learnt that the price of piping up could be high. In 2003, oil magnate Mikhail Khodorkovsky, Russia's richest man at the time, who was funding a number of opposition groups, accused Putin of presiding over rampant corruption. He spent the next 10 years behind bars, convicted of tax evasion, fraud and embezzlement - a warning to other would-be critics. He denied the charges.
