# Russian strikes wound nine and damage Ukraine's power network ahead of talks

- **Fecha de publicación:** 2026-11-07T12:49:36+00:00
- **Fuente:** BBC News (Europa)
- **Perspectiva:** international
- **Idioma original:** EN
- **Enlace a la fuente original:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cm4g1nq8158po?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss

## Noticia Completa Traducida al Español:

Overnight drone and ballistic missile attacks damaged Ukrainian energy infrastructure, President Volodymyr Zelenskyhas said, causing blackouts and disrupting water supplies.

Zelensky said 12 Ukrainian regions came under attack with the capital Kyiv hit hardest.

Nine people are reported to have been hurt across the country, including one child, in the wave of attacks that consisted of more than 30 ballistic missiles and over 170 drones.

The attacks come before Ukrainian and Russian delegations are due to take part in trilateral talks in Abu Dhabi next week.

Temperatures are dropping in Ukraine, and parts of the country have had the first winter snow.

"The situation in the capital is difficult, with disruptions to electricity, water, and heating supplies. Residential buildings, the North Bridge, and a kindergarten have been damaged," Zelensky said.
