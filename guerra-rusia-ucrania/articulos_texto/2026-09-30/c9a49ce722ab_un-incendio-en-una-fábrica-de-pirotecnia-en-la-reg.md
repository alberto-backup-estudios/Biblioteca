### Fire at Pyrotechnics Factory in Yaroslavl Region Kills 14

Fourteen people were killed in a fire at a pyrotechnics factory in the Yaroslavl region on Monday, state media reported, citing emergency services.

One person was being treated for their injuries at the hospital. TASS reported that the fire had been put out.

The blaze engulfed two buildings in the village of Kubrinsk near the town of Pereslavl-Zalessky. Around 200 emergency personnel were deployed to the scene, Evrayev said.

Law enforcement authorities launched a criminal investigation into possible industrial safety violations, TASS reported.
