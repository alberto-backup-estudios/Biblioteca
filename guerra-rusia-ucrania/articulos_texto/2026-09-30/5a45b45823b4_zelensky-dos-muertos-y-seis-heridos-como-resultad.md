## Noticia Completa Traducida al Español:

Dos personas murieron y otras seis resultaron heridas, incluidos tres niños, como resultado de un ataque aéreo ruso en el centro de Sumy, dijo el presidente ucraniano Volodymyr Zelenskyy.

"Cuatro bombas KAB cayeron en el centro de la ciudad, alcanzando un edificio escolar donde se impartían clases. Agradezco a los profesores y a la administración de la escuela: los niños y los profesores estaban en un refugio, y un total de 100 personas fueron evacuadas a tiempo", dijo Zelenskyy en su canal Telegram el martes.

Según él, el sector residencial también sufrió daños.

"Hasta ahora, lamentablemente, sabemos de dos personas que han muerto. Mi más sentido pésame a sus familiares y seres queridos. Seis personas resultaron heridas, entre ellas tres niños", dijo el presidente.

"Los terroristas rusos no son más que monstruos completos. Atacar así a niños, escuelas y hogares comunes. Estados Unidos, Europa y todos nuestros socios no deben olvidar que Rusia no detendrá esta locura sin una presión real", dijo el presidente.

Añadió que Ucrania espera que haya acciones concretas en respuesta a estos ataques, no sólo palabras.

## Texto Original (EN):

### Zelenskyy: Two killed, six wounded as result of Russian airstrike on school in Sumy

Two people were killed and six others were injured, including three children, as a result of a Russian airstrike on the center of Sumy, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has said.

"Four KAB bombs struck the city center, hitting a school building where classes were in session. I thank the teachers and school administration: the children and teachers were in a shelter, and a total of 100 people were evacuated in time," Zelenskyy said on his Telegram channel on Tuesday.

According to him, the residential sector also sustained damage.

"As of now, unfortunately, we know of two people who have died. My condolences to their families and loved ones. Six people were wounded, including three children," the president said.

"Russian terrorists are nothing but complete monsters. To strike like this at children, schools, and ordinary homes. America, Europe, and all our partners must not forget that Russia will not stop this madness without real pressure," the president said.

He added that Ukraine expects that there will be concrete actions in response to these attacks, not just words.
