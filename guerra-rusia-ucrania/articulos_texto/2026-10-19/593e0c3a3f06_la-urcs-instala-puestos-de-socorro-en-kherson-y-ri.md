## Noticia Completa Traducida al Español:

La Sociedad de la Cruz Roja Ucraniana (URCS) ha instalado puestos de socorro para las víctimas de los ataques aéreos rusos que afectaron a Kherson y Rivne a finales de semana.

"Kherson. Los voluntarios de la unidad de respuesta rápida de la Cruz Roja Ucraniana trabajaron en el lugar del ataque en coordinación con otros servicios de emergencia... También se instaló en el lugar una estación de apoyo de la URCS, donde las víctimas y los representantes de los servicios de emergencia pudieron recibir bebidas calientes", informó la URCS en Facebook.

## Texto Original (EN):

### URCS sets up aid stations in Kherson, Rivne

The Ukrainian Red Cross Society (URCS) has set up aid stations for victims of Russian air strikes that hit Kherson and Rivne late in the week.

"Kherson. Volunteers from the rapid response unit of the Ukrainian Red Cross Society worked at the site of the attack in coordination with other emergency services... A URCS support station was also set up on-site, where victims and representatives of emergency services could receive hot drinks," the URCS reported on Facebook.

Volunteers rendered first aid to three injured persons and provided psychological first aid to 15 people suffering from acute stress response.

In Rivne, volunteers at the URCS aid station also provided psychological support to victims. There, people could rest, warm up, have a hot drink, a snack, and charge their devices.

According to the State Emergency Service (SES) of Ukraine, on the night leading into Saturday, the Russian army dropped eight guided aerial bombs on Kherson. As a result of the attack, one person was killed and 21 were injured, including two children. Ten apartment buildings were damaged. Seven people were rescued from under the rubble.
