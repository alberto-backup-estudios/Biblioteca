## Noticia Completa Traducida al Español:

Las tropas rusas bombardearon la región de Jersón, hiriendo a siete personas, informó el jefe de la administración militar regional, Oleksandr Prokudin.

"Debido a la agresión rusa, 7 personas resultaron heridas", escribió en Telegram.

Prokudin señaló que durante el último día Antonivka, Zelenivka, Sadove, Komyshany, Bilozerka, Stanislav, Tomyna Balka, Chereshenky, Dniprovske, Doslidne, Veletenske, Novodmytrivka, Dariivka, Chaichyne, Lymanets, Inhulivka, Vitrove, Ponyativka, Mykilske, Zarichne, Inhulets, Yasna Poliana, Ulianivka, Fedorivka, Tokarivka, Ivanivka, Novotiahynka, Beryslav, Novoraisk, Kostyrka, Novovorontsovka, Borozienske, Urozhaine, Osokorivka, Havrylivka, Dudchany, Mykhailivka, Novokairy, Respublikanets, Sablukivka, Ukrainka, Tomaryne y la ciudad de Kherson quedaron bajo el control de la aviación y los drones enemigos. terror y bombardeos de artillería.

Según él, el ejército ruso atacó infraestructuras críticas y sociales, así como áreas residenciales de los asentamientos de la región, dañando dos edificios de varios pisos y seis casas privadas.

Los ocupantes también destruyeron una iglesia, un museo, una restauración pública, una sucursal bancaria, un edificio administrativo, un supermercado, un gasoducto, un taxi de ruta y coches particulares.

Mientras tanto, el domingo nueve personas fueron evacuadas de las comunidades liberadas de la región.

## Texto Original (EN):

### Enemy shells Kherson region, wounding 7

Russian troops have shelled Kherson region, injuring seven people, Head of the Regional Military Administration Oleksandr Prokudin has reported.

"Due to Russian aggression, 7 people were wounded," he wrote on Telegram.

Prokudin noted that over the past day, Antonivka, Zelenivka, Sadove, Komyshany, Bilozerka, Stanislav, Tomyna Balka, Chereshenky, Dniprovske, Doslidne, Veletenske, Novodmytrivka, Dariivka, Chaichyne, Lymanets, Inhulivka, Vitrove, Ponyativka, Mykilske, Zarichne, Inhulets, Yasna Poliana, Ulianivka, Fedorivka, Tokarivka, Ivanivka, Novotiahynka, Beryslav, Novoraisk, Kostyrka, Novovorontsovka, Borozienske, Urozhaine, Osokorivka, Havrylivka, Dudchany, Mykhailivka, Novokairy, Respublikanets, Sablukivka, Ukrainka, Tomaryne, and the city of Kherson came under enemy aviation, drone terror, and artillery shelling.

According to him, the Russian military targeted critical and social infrastructure, as well as residential areas of the region's settlements, damaging two multi-story buildings and six private houses.

The occupiers also wrecked a church, a museum, a public catering establishment, a bank branch, an administrative building, a supermarket, a gas pipeline, a route taxi, and private cars.

Meanwhile, nine people were evacuated from liberated communities of the region on Sunday.
