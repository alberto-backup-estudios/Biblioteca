## Texto Original (EN):

### NYT: Western intelligence suggests Putin intends to continue war at least until end of winter

Western intelligence assessments indicate that Russian leader Vladimir Putin is unwilling to engage in serious negotiations to reach a peaceful settlement of the Russia-Ukraine war, despite preparing to meet US President Donald Trump in China on Thursday 19 November.

Source: The New York Times, citing officials familiar with the intelligence assessments

Details: The sources told the NYT that the latest Western intelligence assessments highlight Putin's desire to continue fighting in Ukraine at least until the end of winter. The Kremlin leader hopes that his army can make further gains in Ukraine's east or that Russian drone and missile attacks on Kyiv, which have killed civilians, will wear down Ukrainian resistance.

The NYT's sources said Putin is under growing pressure as Russia suffers heavy losses, struggles to mobilise troops and grapples with economic instability. Some added that the war enables him to suppress criticism at home.

These assessments are consistent with recent comments by European diplomats and leaders who have expressed doubts that the full-scale war, which has been ongoing for almost five years, will end any time soon.

"We believe Russia is in a very dire situation," Aurélien Lechevallier, the French ambassador to Washington, said in an interview, referring to the country's wartime economic condition and its impact on Putin's legitimacy. "He needs the war in Ukraine to fuel his own leadership, his own narrative."
