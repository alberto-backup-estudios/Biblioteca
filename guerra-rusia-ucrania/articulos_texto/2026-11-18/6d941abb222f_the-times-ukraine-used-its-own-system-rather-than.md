## Texto Original (EN):

### The Times: Ukraine used its own system rather than Starlink in Crimean Bridge attack – video

Ukraine did not rely on Starlink or Starshield during its attack on the Crimean Bridge on 17 November, instead using its own satellite system.

Source: The Times, citing Ukrainian sources, as reported by Ukrainian media outlet hromadske

Details: An unnamed Ukrainian officer involved in planning the operation told The Times that the strike on the bridge demonstrates that Ukraine no longer needs the Starlink and Starshield networks to carry out strike missions.

Quote: "For the first time in history we used all these drones to pass video signal to missiles so they could hit precise points on the bridge. We showed we can conduct such operations without Starlink."

Details: The officer also described the strike on the bridge as a "symbolic gesture" ahead of the meeting between the leaders of the US, Russia and China, intended "to show that Putin is weak".

The Times, citing its sources, reported that the operation had been carried out as a complex, combined attack.
