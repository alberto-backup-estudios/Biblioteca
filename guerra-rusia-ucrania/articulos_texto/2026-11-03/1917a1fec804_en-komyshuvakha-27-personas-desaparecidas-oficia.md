## Noticia Completa Traducida al Español:

En Komyshuvakha, región de Zaporizhia, 27 personas están desaparecidas tras un ataque ruso el lunes, según el jefe de la administración militar regional de Zaporizhzhia, Mykhailo Semikin.

"Dieciocho personas murieron, entre ellas un policía y dos socorristas del Servicio Estatal de Emergencias. Cinco resultaron heridas. Otras 27 personas se consideran desaparecidas", dijo Semikin en su canal Telegram.

En el lugar continúan las operaciones de búsqueda y rescate.

Como se informó anteriormente, los ocupantes rusos llevaron a cabo ataques de precisión en el centro de Komyshuvakha utilizando al menos nueve bombas aéreas guiadas FAB-3000. Entre los objetivos alcanzados se encontraba una escuela donde se distribuía ayuda humanitaria. "Después de la primera oleada de ataques aéreos, hubo una segunda oleada de nueve bombas más, dirigidas al mismo refugio donde se refugiaba la gente", dijo el presidente ucraniano Volodymyr Zelenskyy.

## Texto Original (EN):

### In Komyshuvakha, 27 people missing; police officer, two rescuers among dead

In Komyshuvakha, Zaporizhia region, 27 people are missing following a Russian strike on Monday, according to Head of Zaporizhzhia Regional Military Administration Mykhailo Semikin.

"Eighteen people were killed, including a police officer and two State Emergency Service rescuers. Five were wounded. Another 27 people are considered missing," Semikin said on his Telegram channel.

Search and rescue operations are ongoing at the site.

As previously reported, Russian occupiers carried out precision strikes on the center of Komyshuvakha using at least nine FAB-3000 guided aerial bombs. Among the targets hit was a school where humanitarian aid was being distributed. "After the first wave of airstrikes, there was a second wave of nine more bombs, targeting the very shelter where people were taking refuge," Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said.
