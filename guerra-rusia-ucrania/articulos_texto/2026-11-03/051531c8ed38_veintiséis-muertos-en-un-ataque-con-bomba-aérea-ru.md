## Noticia Completa Traducida al Español:

Las fuerzas rusas bombardearon una escuela utilizada para distribuir ayuda humanitaria en Komyshuvakha, en la provincia de Zaporizhzhia. Se sabe que veintiséis personas, entre ellas agentes de policía y socorristas, murieron, cinco resultaron heridas y 27 fueron reportadas como desaparecidas.

Fuente: Presidente Volodymyr Zelenskyy en X (Twitter); Mykhailo Semikin, jefe de la administración militar de la provincia de Zaporizhzhia; Servicio Estatal de Emergencia de Ucrania

Detalles: Zelenskyy dijo que después del ataque aéreo inicial con bomba, los rusos lanzaron un ataque repetido con nueve bombas más, alcanzando el refugio donde la gente se refugiaba.

"Trágicamente, ya se ha informado de la muerte de 18 personas. Mi más sentido pésame a sus familiares y seres queridos. Cinco personas resultaron heridas. Se está llevando a cabo una operación de rescate en el lugar y la búsqueda de personas continúa. Es extremadamente difícil. Están involucradas fuerzas militares, socorristas y servicios especiales", informó el presidente.

Zelenskyy dijo que las fuerzas rusas no podían haber ignorado que cada semana se distribuía ayuda humanitaria en la escuela a los residentes locales que habían permanecido en Komyshuvakha.

## Texto Original (EN):

### Twenty-six killed in Russian aerial bomb attack on school used to distribute humanitarian aid in Zaporizhzhia Oblast – photos, video

Russian forces have bombed a school used to distribute humanitarian aid in Komyshuvakha, Zaporizhzhia Oblast. Twenty-six people, including police officers and first responders, are known to have been killed, five injured and 27 reported missing.

Source: President Volodymyr Zelenskyy on X (Twitter); Mykhailo Semikin, Head of Zaporizhzhia Oblast Military Administration; Ukraine's State Emergency Service

Quote from Zelenskyy: "An utterly horrific Russian attack on the settlement of Komyshuvakha in the Zaporizhzhia region. Since this morning, the settlement has been hit several times by aerial bombs. The strikes targeted a school used for humanitarian purposes – people were receiving humanitarian aid there."

Details: Zelenskyy said that after the initial aerial bomb strike, the Russians launched a repeat attack with nine more bombs, hitting the shelter where people were taking cover.

"Tragically, 18 people have already been reported killed. My condolences to their families and loved ones. Five people have been injured. A rescue operation is underway at the site, and the search for people continues. It is extremely difficult. Military forces, first responders, and special services are involved," the president reported.

Zelenskyy said the Russian forces could not have been unaware that humanitarian aid was distributed at the school every week to local residents who had remained in Komyshuvakha.
