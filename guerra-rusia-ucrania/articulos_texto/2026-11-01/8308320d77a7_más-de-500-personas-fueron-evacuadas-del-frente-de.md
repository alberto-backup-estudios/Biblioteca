## Noticia Completa Traducida al Español:

Durante el último día, los ocupantes rusos bombardearon asentamientos en la región de Donetsk 23 veces, lo que provocó la muerte de dos civiles, informó el jefe de la Administración Militar Regional, Vadym Filashkin.

"En Slaviansk murieron 2 personas y 12 resultaron heridas; 30 casas privadas, un edificio de apartamentos, una gasolinera, una sucursal de correos, cuartos de servicio y 2 coches resultaron dañados", escribió el sábado en Telegram.

Según la administración militar regional, en Kramatorsk dos personas resultaron heridas y dos casas particulares y un automóvil sufrieron daños. En Druzhkivka, 2 personas resultaron heridas.

En total, en las últimas 24 horas, los rusos bombardearon asentamientos en la región de Donetsk 23 veces. Unas 519 personas, entre ellas 44 niños, fueron evacuadas del frente.

## Texto Original (EN):

### Over 500 evacuated from Donetsk region frontline in past day – official

Over the past day, Russian occupiers shelled settlements in Donetsk region 23 times, resulting in the deaths of two civilians, Head of the Regional Military Administration Vadym Filashkin reported.

"In Slovyansk, 2 people were killed and 12 were injured; 30 private houses, an apartment building, a gas station, a post office branch, utility rooms, and 2 cars were damaged," he wrote on Telegram on Saturday.

According to the Regional Military Administration, 2 people were injured in Kramatorsk, and 2 private houses and a car were damaged. In Druzhkivka, 2 people were injured.

In total, over the past 24 hours, the Russians shelled settlements in Donetsk region 23 times. Some 519 people, including 44 children, were evacuated from the frontline.
