### From a drone-downing pastry chef to the company commander who carried a wounded soldier for miles on foot: 10 stories of Ukraine's defenders

2026 has been a year of victories great and small for the Ukrainian military, and the whole country has celebrated along with them.

Nazar Daletskyi, a serviceman believed to have been killed three years ago – a funeral had even been held for him – came home alive from captivity.

Vladyslav Polskyi, a company commander in the 475th Assault Regiment (Code 9.2), single-handedly evacuated two wounded soldiers in a civilian pickup truck as Russian drones circled overhead.

The National Guard awarded the title of Hero of Ukraine to a woman for the first time – machine gunner Oleksandra Davydenko.

Ukraine's defence forces in the south reclaimed up to 435 sq km of territory. The 3rd Army Corps stunned the public with the successful Operation Vivaldi counteroffensive in Donetsk Oblast.
