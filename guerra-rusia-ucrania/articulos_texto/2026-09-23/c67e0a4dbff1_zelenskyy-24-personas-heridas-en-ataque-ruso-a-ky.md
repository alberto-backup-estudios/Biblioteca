## Noticia Completa Traducida al Español:

El presidente ucraniano, Volodymyr Zelenskyy, ha informado que 24 personas resultaron heridas en un ataque ruso en Kiev y que las fuerzas rusas continúan atacando infraestructura civil y crítica.

"Desde esta mañana, se ha estado trabajando para hacer frente a las consecuencias de los ataques rusos en Kiev. Los rusos lanzaron muchos drones contra la ciudad, un número significativo de ellos propulsados ​​por aviones", dijo Zelenskyy en una publicación publicada en Facebook el miércoles.

Según él, Rusia continúa apuntando a infraestructuras civiles y críticas, específicamente instalaciones farmacéuticas, gasolineras, ferrocarriles, infraestructuras de comunicaciones y centros comerciales.

"Hasta el momento se reportan dos personas muertas y otras 24 heridas. Mi más sentido pésame a sus familiares y seres queridos", señaló el Presidente.

También informó que se registraron huelgas contra ciudades y comunidades en ocho regiones.

"En cada reunión de la Asamblea General de la ONU, trabajamos para garantizar el apoyo continuo a Ucrania. Igualmente importante es la presión justa ejercida sobre Rusia a través de sanciones", subrayó Zelenskyy.

## Texto Original (EN):

### Zelenskyy: 24 people injured in Russian attack on Kyiv; Russia turning civilian and critical infrastructure into targets

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has reported that 24 people were injured in a Russian strike on Kyiv, and that Russian forces continue to target civilian and critical infrastructure.

“Since this morning, work has been underway to deal with the aftermath of Russian strikes on Kyiv. The Russians launched many drones against the city, a significant number of them jet-powered,” Zelenskyy said in a post published on Facebook Wednesday.

According to him, Russia continues to target civilian and critical infrastructure, specifically pharmaceutical facilities, gas stations, railways, communications infrastructure, and shopping centers.

“As of now, two people have been reported killed and another 24 wounded. My condolences to their families and loved ones,” the President noted.

He also reported that strikes were recorded against cities and communities in eight regions.

“At every meeting during the UN General Assembly, we are working to ensure continued support for Ukraine. Equally important is the fair pressure exerted on Russia through sanctions,” Zelenskyy stressed.
