## Noticia Completa Traducida al Español:

El presidente Volodymyr Zelenskyy ha dicho que Ucrania transfirió recientemente a dos prisioneros de guerra norcoreanos a Corea del Sur.

Fuente: Discurso de Zelenskyy ante la Asamblea General de la ONU el 23 de septiembre

Cita: "Recientemente enviamos a dos prisioneros de guerra norcoreanos a la República de Corea... Uno de ellos, cuando se dio cuenta de que lo iban a hacer prisionero, intentó suicidarse y se sorprendió de que el destino no lo dejara morir y así es como crían a la gente en el Norte [Corea]. Es la realidad. Les quitan la libertad. Les quitan el derecho a elegir y les inculcan que deben morir, pase lo que pase".

Detalles: Zelenskyy añadió que Rusia está utilizando a ciudadanos de 47 países en su guerra, incluidos Ghana, Nepal, Tayikistán, India, Yemen, Kenia, Zimbabwe, Cuba, Bielorrusia, Sudán y Sudáfrica.

Cita: "Quiero hablar directamente con cada representante de estos 47 países. Ustedes saben lo que Rusia está haciendo con su gente. Son sus ciudadanos. Cuídenlos y no dejen que terminen en otro frente. No dejen que Putin los arrastre a esta guerra. No dejen que Rusia apague sus incendios, cubra su vergüenza y pague su locura con las vidas de su pueblo".

## Texto Original (EN):

### Zelenskyy says Ukraine transferred two North Korean prisoners of war to South Korea

President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has said Ukraine recently transferred two North Korean prisoners of war to South Korea.

Source: Zelenskyy's address to the UN General Assembly on 23 September

Quote: "Recently we sent two North Korean prisoners of war to the Republic of Korea… One of them – when he realised he was going to be taken prisoner – tried to kill himself and he was shocked that fate didn't let him die and this is how they raise people in the North [Korea]. It's reality. They take away their freedom. They take away their right to choose and they drill into them that they must die. no matter what."

Details: Zelenskyy added that Russia is using citizens of 47 countries in its war, including Ghana, Nepal, Tajikistan, India, Yemen, Kenya, Zimbabwe, Cuba, Belarus, Sudan and South Africa.

Quote: "I want to speak directly to every representative of these 47 countries. You know what Russia is doing with your people. It's your citizens. Take care of them and do not let them end up on another front. Do not let Putin drag you into this war. Do not let Russia put out its fires, cover up its shame, and pay for its madness with the lives of your people."
