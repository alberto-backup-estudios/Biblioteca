## Texto Original (EN):

### Russia deploy electronic-warfare-immune fibre-optic Molniya drones across various frontlines – video

Over the past few days, the use of Russian Molniya unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) controlled via fibre-optic cable has been recorded across various sectors of the frontline.

Source: Serhii "Flash" Beskrestnov, an adviser to Ukraine's defence minister and a radio technology specialist

Quote: "The flight altitude is approximately 50 m, with a range of up to 30 km into our rear."

Details: Beskrestnov said that the first attempts to equip Molniya UAVs with fibre optics were recorded a year ago, though the use of such drones did not become widespread at that time.

Quote: "The danger of such UAVs lies in the absence of a radio signal to detect them, their low flight altitude (which complicates radar detection), the ability to conduct reconnaissance during flight thanks to high image quality, and the inability of electronic warfare to affect the UAV."
