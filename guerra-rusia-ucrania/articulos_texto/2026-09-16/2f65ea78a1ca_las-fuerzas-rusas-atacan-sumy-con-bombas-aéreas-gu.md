## Noticia Completa en Español:

Las fuerzas rusas atacaron los distritos de Kovpakivskyi y Zarichnyi de Sumy con bombas aéreas guiadas, hiriendo a seis personas.

Fuente: Oleh Hryhorov, Jefe de la Administración Militar del Óblast de Sumy; Artem Kobzar, alcalde en funciones de Sumy

Detalles: "Actualmente se sabe que seis personas resultaron heridas en los bombardeos aéreos dirigidos sobre Sumy", dijo Hryhorov.

Uno de los heridos es una niña de 14 años.

Los rusos atacaron zonas residenciales y la zona industrial de la ciudad.

## Texto Original (EN):

### Russian forces strike Sumy with guided aerial bombs: six injured, including teenager

Russian forces have struck the Kovpakivskyi and Zarichnyi districts of Sumy with guided aerial bombs, injuring six people.

Source: Oleh Hryhorov , Head of Sumy Oblast Military Administration; Artem Kobzar , acting mayor of Sumy

Details: "Six people are currently known to have been injured in the guided aerial bomb strikes on Sumy," Hryhorov said.

One of those injured is a 14-year-old girl.

The Russians attacked residential areas and the city's industrial zone.

The strikes destroyed two houses and damaged another 10 homes and an educational institution.
