## Noticia Completa en Español:

Un ataque ruso a gran escala ha provocado nuevos cortes de energía en seis regiones de Ucrania.

Fuente: Ukrenergo, operador estatal de transmisión de electricidad de Ucrania.

Detalles: Las regiones afectadas son las provincias de Sumy, Cherkasy, Donetsk, Zaporizhzhia, Kherson y Kharkiv.

"Los trabajos de reparación de emergencia ya están en marcha siempre que las condiciones de seguridad lo permitan. Los ingenieros energéticos están trabajando para reparar los equipos dañados por las fuerzas rusas y reanudar su funcionamiento lo antes posible", dijo Ukrenergo.

El consumo de electricidad ha disminuido. A las 09:30 horas del 10 de septiembre, era un 7% más bajo que el día anterior.

## Texto Original (EN):

### Russians attack energy infrastructure in six regions

A large-scale Russian attack has caused new power outages in six Ukrainian regions.

Source: Ukrenergo , Ukraine's state-owned electricity transmission operator

Details: The affected regions are Sumy, Cherkasy, Donetsk, Zaporizhzhia, Kherson and Kharkiv oblasts.

"Emergency repair work is already underway wherever safety conditions currently allow. Power engineers are working to repair equipment damaged by Russian forces and resume its operation as soon as possible," Ukrenergo said.

Electricity consumption has decreased. As of 09:30 on 10 September, it was 7% lower than on the previous day.

Power engineers are asking consumers to shift their active use of electricity to 11:00-15:00.

Background: A power line has been damaged by debris from Russian aerial assets, leaving 18 settlements in Mykolaiv Oblast without electricity .
