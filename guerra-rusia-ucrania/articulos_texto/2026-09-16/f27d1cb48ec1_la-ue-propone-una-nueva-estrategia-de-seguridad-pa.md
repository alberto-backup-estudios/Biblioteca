## Noticia Completa en Español:

En respuesta a la intensificación de los ataques híbridos de Rusia contra Europa, la presidenta de la Comisión Europea, Ursula von der Leyen, ha anunciado planes para una nueva estrategia de seguridad para los países europeos que complemente el Artículo 4 de la OTAN.

Fuente: von der Leyen durante su discurso anual ante el Parlamento Europeo el 16 de septiembre, según informó European Pravda

Cita: "A medida que aumenta el nivel de amenaza, también debe hacerlo la preparación de Europa. Por eso propondré, junto con la Alta Representante [Kaja Kallas – ed.], una nueva estrategia de seguridad europea.

Creo que es hora de adoptar el propio Artículo 4 de Europa, o un Protocolo de Seguridad de Emergencia. Cuando lo activara un estado miembro, todos los estados miembros se reunirían. Coordinar una respuesta europea. Para disuadir una mayor escalada. Y para mitigar el impacto."

Detalles: Von der Leyen señaló que los ciberataques, los sabotajes, los incendios provocados y las incursiones con drones en la UE se intensifican cada día.

## Texto Original (EN):

### EU proposes new security strategy to complement NATO's Article 4 amid Russian attacks

In response to Russia's intensifying hybrid attacks on Europe, European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen has announced plans for a new security strategy for European countries to complement NATO's Article 4.

Source: von der Leyen during her annual address to the European Parliament on 16 September, as reported by European Pravda

Quote: " As the threat level climbs, so must Europe's preparedness. This is why I will propose, together with the HRVP [Kaja Kallas – ed.] , a new European security strategy.

I believe it is time for Europe's own Article 4 – or an Emergency Security Protocol. When triggered by one member state, all member states would convene. To coordinate a European response. To deter further escalation. And to mitigate the impact."

Details: Von der Leyen noted that cyberattacks, sabotage, arson and drone incursions into the EU are intensifying every day.

She stressed that Europe needs its own response plan for hybrid threats.

"We urgently need a consensus on how to respond to certain incidents. Those which fall below armed aggression but clearly threaten our national security. In other words, a mechanism to flank NATO's Article 4 ," the Commission president said.

For reference: NATO's Article 4 enables any member state to request consultations with its allies if it considers that its territorial integrity is under threat. It is considered a preliminary step towards invoking NATO's Article 5.

In August, members of German Chancellor Friedrich Merz's party called for NATO Treaty Article 4 consultations after a drone was spotted near a Ukrainian aircraft in Leipzig.
