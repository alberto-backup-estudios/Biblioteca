## Noticia Completa en Español:

El Ministro de Asuntos Exteriores de Ucrania, Andrii Sybiha, ha dicho que el plan del gobernante del Kremlin, Vladimir Putin, es provocar una nueva ola de migración a Europa a través de una crisis alimentaria provocada por el bloqueo de las exportaciones ucranianas a través del Mar Negro.

Fuente: Sybiha en X (Twitter), según lo informado por la Pravda europea

Detalles: Sybiha dijo que el terrorismo ruso también amenaza a países de África, Medio Oriente y Asia.

Añadió que Moscú planea desestabilizar Europa mediante acciones similares.

Cita: " Putin apuesta a que el hambre y la escasez de alimentos pueden provocar una afluencia de inmigrantes. Eso es precisamente lo que pretende, especialmente antes de las elecciones en los estados miembros de la UE el próximo año. Putin sabe que la migración es uno de los temas más delicados y divisivos en las campañas electorales europeas, especialmente después de ver con qué eficacia las redes vinculadas a Rusia explotaron la reciente crisis de Ceuta. ## Texto Original (EN):

### Ukrainian foreign minister: Putin seeks to trigger migration to Europe by causing food crisis

Ukrainian Foreign Minister Andrii Sybiha has said Kremlin ruler Vladimir Putin's plan is to provoke a new wave of migration to Europe through a food crisis caused by the blockade of Ukrainian exports via the Black Sea.

Source: Sybiha on X (Twitter) , as reported by European Pravda

Details: Sybiha said Russian terrorism also threatens countries in Africa, the Middle East and Asia.

He added that Moscow plans to destabilise Europe through similar actions.

Quote: " Putin is betting that hunger and food shortages can cause an influx of migrants. That is precisely what he aims for, particularly ahead of elections in EU member states next year. Putin knows that migration is one of the most sensitive and divisive issues in European election campaigns – especially after seeing how effectively Russian-linked networks exploited the recent Ceuta crisis. "

Details: Sybiha believes that Putin will use all available methods to increase migration to Europe, with the blockade of the Black Sea being just one of them. Another is the Lukashenko regime in Belarus, he added.

Quote: " Moscow believes its strategy will create tension, sow division, strengthen radical anti-European movements, and ultimately weaken Europe's support for Ukraine. That is why supporting Ukraine's agricultural exports and unblocking freedom of navigation in the Black Sea are of strategic and existential value for the European Union, as well as the wider international community."

Details: In this context, the minister said urgent measures are needed to ensure uninterrupted commercial shipping from Ukrainian seaports, develop all possible alternative routes and mechanisms for transporting cargo through the EU, and provide maximum support to Ukrainian farmers.
