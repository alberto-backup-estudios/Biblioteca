### 8 Killed Across Ukraine as Russia Strikes Bridges in Kyiv

A Russian ballistic missile attack struck three bridges on Kyiv's Dnipro River, the city's mayor said on Friday, in what was the biggest attack on routes connecting the left and right banks of Ukraine's capital since the start of the war.

The strikes, which also caused fires and damaged critical infrastructure and buildings, come as Russian President Vladimir Putin promised to hit back hard after Ukraine hit the bridge linking Russia and Crimea for a third time this week. A Reuters witness in Kyiv heard a series of explosions in the early hours of Friday.

Reuters could not immediately confirm the reports. Russia denies deliberately targeting civilians.

The strikes follow warnings from European allies on Thursday against any attempt to decide a peace settlement over their heads, after President Donald Trump embraced Russia's Vladimir Putin as a “friend” at a summit with Chinese leader Xi Jinping.
