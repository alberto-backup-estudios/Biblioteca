## Texto Original (EN):

### Russia attacks Ukraine with three ballistic missiles and 148 drones, hits recorded at 19 locations

Russian forces attacked Ukraine with three ballistic missiles and 148 drones (87 jet-powered) on the night of 20-21 November. Air defence forces have destroyed 117 UAVs.

Details: The Russians launched three Iskander-M/KN-23/S-400 ballistic missiles from Bryansk Oblast and temporarily occupied Crimea.

The Russians also deployed 148 Shahed, Gerbera, Parodiya and other types of drones from the Russian cities of Oryol, Bryansk, Primorsko-Akhtarsk and Millerovo as well as from temporarily occupied Donetsk and Hvardiiske in Crimea.

As of 08:00, Ukrainian air defence forces had destroyed or jammed 117 Russian drones (67 jet-powered).

Hits by missiles and drones have been recorded at 19 locations and the fall of downed UAVs (debris) at nine locations.
