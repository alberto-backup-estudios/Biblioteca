## Texto Original (EN):

### Ukrainian air defences down 87 Russian drones overnight, strikes recorded at 20 locations

Russian forces attacked Ukraine with ballistic missiles and 113 drones on the night of 25-26 November. Air defences have destroyed or jammed 87 UAVs.

Details: Starting at 18:00 on 25 November, the Russians attacked Ukraine with Iskander-M/Zircon/S-400 ballistic missiles from Russia's Bryansk and Kursk oblasts as well as 113 Shahed (76 jet-powered) and Gerbera attack drones and Parodiya decoy UAVs. The drones were launched from the Russian cities of Oryol, Bryansk, Primorsko-Akhtarsk and Millerovo as well as from temporarily occupied Donetsk and Hvardiiske in Crimea.

Aircraft, anti-aircraft missile units, electronic warfare assets, unmanned systems units and mobile fire groups from Ukraine's defence forces have been involved in repelling the attack.

Early reports indicated that as of 08:00, Ukrainian air defence forces had destroyed or jammed 87 Shahed (52 jet-powered), Gerbera and other types of drones.

Hits by drones have been recorded at 20 locations and the fall of downed UAVs (debris) at six locations.
