## Noticia Completa Traducida al Español:

Tres personas murieron y siete resultaron heridas en los ataques rusos contra las provincias de Kherson, Dnipropetrovsk y Donetsk durante el último día.

Fuente: Administración Militar del Óblast de Kherson; Administración Militar del Óblast de Dnipropetrovsk; Administración militar del Óblast de Donetsk

Detalles: Dos personas murieron y cinco resultaron heridas en los ataques rusos al Óblast de Kherson.

Los rusos atacaron instalaciones de infraestructura social y crítica, así como áreas residenciales en todo el oblast. Tres edificios residenciales de gran altura, cinco casas, una oficina de correos, una dependencia y una ambulancia resultaron dañados.

Las fuerzas rusas atacaron el distrito de Nikopol en el óblast de Dnipropetrovsk casi diez veces utilizando drones y artillería. Un hombre de 34 años y una mujer de 57 resultaron heridos en Marhanets 
hromada

. Ambos reciben tratamiento ambulatorio. Los edificios de apartamentos resultaron dañados.

## Texto Original (EN):

### Three killed and seven injured in Russian attacks on three Ukrainian oblasts

Three people were killed and seven injured in Russian attacks on Kherson, Dnipropetrovsk and Donetsk oblasts over the past day.

Source: Kherson Oblast Military Administration; Dnipropetrovsk Oblast Military Administration; Donetsk Oblast Military Administration

Details: Two people were killed and five injured in Russian attacks on Kherson Oblast.

The Russians targeted critical and social infrastructure facilities as well as residential areas across the oblast. Three high-rise residential buildings, five houses, a post office, an outbuilding and an ambulance were damaged.

Russian forces attacked the Nikopol district in Dnipropetrovsk Oblast almost 10 times using drones and artillery. A 34-year-old man and a 57-year-old woman were injured in the Marhanets 
hromada

. Both are receiving outpatient treatment. Apartment buildings were damaged.

One person was killed in Russian attacks on Yasna Poliana in Donetsk Oblast.
