## Noticia Completa Traducida al Español:

Dos personas murieron y otras tres resultaron heridas como resultado del bombardeo ruso de los asentamientos en la región de Donetsk, con 24 ataques registrados durante el último día, informó el miércoles el jefe de la Administración Militar Regional de Donetsk, Vadym Filashkin.

Según sus datos, dos personas resultaron heridas en Slaviansk, distrito de Kramatorsk, donde resultaron dañados edificios residenciales de varios pisos, cafeterías, tiendas, una iglesia, una casa privada y locales industriales.

En Novostepanivka de la comunidad territorial de Oleksandrivka resultó dañado un edificio administrativo; en Novobakhmetieve, un almacén; y en Rozdolle, una casa particular.

Una persona murió en Novodonetske, otra murió y otra resultó herida en Iverske.

"En total, durante el último día, los rusos bombardearon los asentamientos en la región de Donetsk 24 veces. 750 personas, entre ellas 82 niños, fueron evacuadas del frente", enfatizó Filashkin en su canal Telegram.

## Texto Original (EN):

### Russian shelling of Donetsk region kills 2, wounds 3 in past day – official

Two people were killed and three others were wounded as a result of Russian shelling of settlements in Donetsk region, with 24 attacks recorded over the past day, Head of the Donetsk Regional Military Administration Vadym Filashkin reported on Wednesday.

According to his data, two people were wounded in Sloviansk, Kramatorsk district, where multi-story residential buildings, cafes, shops, a church, a private house, and industrial premises were damaged.

In Novostepanivka of the Oleksandrivka territorial community, an administrative building was damaged; in Novobakhmetieve, a warehouse; and in Rozdolle, a private house.

One person was killed in Novodonetske, while another person was killed and one more was wounded in Iverske.

"In total, over the past day, the Russians shelled settlements in Donetsk region 24 times. 750 people, including 82 children, were evacuated from the frontline," Filashkin emphasized on his Telegram channel.
