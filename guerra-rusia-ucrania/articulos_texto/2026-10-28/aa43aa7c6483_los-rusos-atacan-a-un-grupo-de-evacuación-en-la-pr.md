### Russians attack evacuation group in Kharkiv Oblast, injuring two police officers – video, photos

Two law enforcement officers were injured as the Russians struck the White Angels police evacuation group in Kharkiv Oblast with an FPV drone on the morning of 28 October.

Quote: "In the morning, the Russians attacked the White Angels police evacuation group, which, together with representatives of the Ukrainian Red Cross Society, was heading to the settlement of Zelenyi Hai in the Velykyi Burluk 
hromada

 of the Kupiansk district to evacuate the civilian population."

Details: Two police officers suffered injuries and are receiving medical treatment. The police car sustained significant damage but remained operational. A car belonging to the humanitarian organisation was also damaged.

Law enforcement officers are documenting the consequences of the attack and collecting evidence of a war crime.

Background: On 28 October, a 60-year-old man was killed and four people were injured when Russian forces struck Kharkiv's Osnovianskyi district with an attack drone.
