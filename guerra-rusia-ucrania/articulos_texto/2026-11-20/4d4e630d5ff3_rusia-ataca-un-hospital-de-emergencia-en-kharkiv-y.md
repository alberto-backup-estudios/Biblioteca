## Texto Original (EN):

### Russia strikes emergency hospital in Kharkiv, damaging MRI scan room – photos

Russian forces have attacked an emergency hospital in Kharkiv. A Russian drone has struck a room housing an MRI scanner. The scanner appears to have remained intact, but specialists will assess whether it is still functioning properly.

Details: The drone attacked the hospital in the city's Shevchenkivskyi district on the morning of 20 November. Four people were injured.

Yevhenii Blahoveshchenskyi, director of the emergency hospital, said damage had been caused to the roof and furniture in the room. Around 100 windows were shattered in total.

"This is a 100% civilian facility – a city hospital that provides medical care to patients from Kharkiv and Kharkiv Oblast. We have absolutely no connection to military facilities," he stressed.

Blahoveshchenskyi said work is underway to save the building and the shattered windows are being boarded up with OSB panels. He hopes the hospital will be back up and running as soon as possible.
