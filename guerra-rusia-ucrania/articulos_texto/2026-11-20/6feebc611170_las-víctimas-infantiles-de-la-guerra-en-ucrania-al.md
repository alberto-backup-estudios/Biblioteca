### Child casualties from war in Ukraine reach highest level in almost four years – UNICEF

In 2026 the number of children killed or injured by the war in Ukraine reached its highest level in almost four years. During the first 10 months of this year, 114 children have been killed and 1,030 injured.

Details: According to the UN Human Rights Monitoring Mission in Ukraine (HRMMU), at least 114 children have been killed and 1,030 injured as a result of the war between January and October 2026. UNICEF noted that this means more than three Ukrainian children are killed or injured every day.

"Last month alone, at least 146 children were killed or injured across Ukraine, a 150 per cent increase when compared to the same period last year," UNICEF reported.

"These latest figures reflect an alarming escalation in violence affecting children in Ukraine. Attacks involving missiles and armed drones continue to put children and families at risk in their homes, schools, and communities, including far from the frontline."

The number of children killed in Ukraine in 2026 has already exceeded the total for 2025 by more than 20%. Meanwhile, the number of children injured has increased by almost 60%.
