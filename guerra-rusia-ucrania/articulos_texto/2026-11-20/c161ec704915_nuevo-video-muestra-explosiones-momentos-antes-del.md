### New video shows explosions moments before part of Crimea Bridge collapses

BBC Verify has analysed new footage showing explosions at the bridge which links Russia and illegally annexed Crimea following an attack by Ukrainian forces.

Ukraine used drones and missiles against the Crimean Bridge on 17 November which brought down a key section of the road.
