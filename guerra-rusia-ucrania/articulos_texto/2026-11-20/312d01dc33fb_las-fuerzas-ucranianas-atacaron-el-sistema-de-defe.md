## Texto Original (EN):

### Ukrainian forces hit a Pantsir defence system that protected Russian sites in Bryansk Oblast

Ukraine's defence forces have conducted strikes against a number of military assets in Russia and in temporarily occupied territories of Ukraine on 19 November and overnight on 19-20 November.

Quote: "In Bryansk Oblast, a Pantsir-S2 anti-aircraft missile and gun system was hit. This system was covering Russian facilities from Ukrainian drones and missiles. Now there is another gap in this defence system."

Details: The General Staff added that a Russian unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) ground control station was struck in the area of Selydove, Donetsk Oblast.

Quote: "From this station, the occupiers guided drones onto the positions of our troops and onto peaceful cities."

Details: Logistics depots of Russian army units were also hit in the areas of Rubizhne and Lysychansk in Luhansk Oblast.
