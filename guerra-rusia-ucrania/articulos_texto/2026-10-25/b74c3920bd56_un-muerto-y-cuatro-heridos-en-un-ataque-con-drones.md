## Noticia Completa Traducida al Español:

Una persona murió y cuatro resultaron heridas en un ataque con aviones no tripulados rusos en el distrito Novobavarskyi de Kharkiv.

### One killed and four injured in Russian drone strike on Kharkiv

One person has been killed and four people have been injured in a Russian drone strike on Kharkiv's Novobavarskyi district.

Source: Kharkiv Mayor Ihor Terekhov on Telegram

Quote: "The Novobavarskyi district has been attacked by an enemy jet-powered drone. We are still assessing the impact."

Details: Terekhov initially reported that three people had been injured.

He later said that one person had been killed and four injured in the strike.
