### Four killed and 14 injured in Russian attacks on Dnipropetrovsk Oblast – video

Four people have been killed and 14 others injured, including an 11-year-old girl, in Russian attacks on Dnipropetrovsk Oblast. Over the past day, Russian forces have attacked three districts of the oblast more than 20 times using drones and an aerial bomb.

Source: Oleksandr Hanzha, Head of Dnipropetrovsk Oblast Military Administration

Details: In the Synelnykove district, the Russians attacked the city of Synelnykove and the Vasylkivka, Slavhorod, Petropavlivka and Mykolaivka 
hromadas

. Houses and an apartment building, shops, infrastructure and cars were damaged.

The strike on Synelnykove on 24 October killed four people and injured 13 others. Among those injured was an 11-year-old girl, who is receiving outpatient treatment.

On the morning of 25 October, it became known that a 62-year-old man who had sustained serious injuries in the attack had died in hospital. Four more of those injured remain in hospital in a serious condition.

The city of Nikopol and the Marhanets, Chervonohryhorivka and Pokrovske hromadas came under attack in the Nikopol district. A house was damaged. A 50-year-old man was taken to hospital in a moderate condition.
