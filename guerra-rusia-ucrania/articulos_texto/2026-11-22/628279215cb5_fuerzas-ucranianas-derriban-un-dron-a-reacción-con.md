### Ukrainian forces down jet-powered drone with a Kalashnikov rifle – video

The Ukrainian military personnel have posted footage showing how they brought down a Russian jet-powered drone using a Kalashnikov assault rifle.

Source: 81st Separate Slobozhanske Airmobile Brigade

Details: The drone was shot down on the Sloviansk front in Donetsk Oblast. Troops from a mobile fire group belonging to the brigade's anti-aircraft missile battalion brought it down with a folding-stock Kalashnikov while on combat duty.

Quote from one of the soldiers: "I shot it down! I shot it down with a rifle, I did!"

Details: The unit did not specify the type of Russian military jet-powered drone that the troops shot down.
