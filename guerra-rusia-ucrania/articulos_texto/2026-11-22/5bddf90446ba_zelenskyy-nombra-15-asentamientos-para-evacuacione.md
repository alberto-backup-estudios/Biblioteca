## Texto Original (EN):

### Zelenskyy names 15 settlements for evacuations similar to Oleshky operation

President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has named 15 settlements in the Russian-occupied parts of Kherson and Zaporizhzhia oblasts where Ukraine is proposing to organise humanitarian missions similar to the evacuation from Oleshky.

Source: Zelenskyy in an audio statement to Ukrainian journalists

Quote: "We are once again grateful to the US first lady for Oleshky, for the evacuation, for lifting the blockade and evacuating our people, citizens of Ukraine.

We discussed the matter with Mykhailo Drapatyi, Commander-in-Chief of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and confirmed the list again today. We had a detailed discussion about these humanitarian missions, the number of people we can evacuate and the plans we have drawn up."

Details: Zelenskyy reported that the updated list of settlements had already been handed over to Ukraine's human rights commissioner and the US side.
