### Zelenskyy: 14 people still unaccounted for after Russian strike on apartment block in Kyiv Oblast – video

A section of a five-storey apartment building in the settlement of Dymеr in Kyiv Oblast has been partially destroyed in a Russian drone strike. President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has reported that 14 people are still unaccounted for and may be trapped under the rubble.

Quote: "Since this morning, a search and rescue operation has been underway in Dymer, Kyiv Oblast. Part of a five-storey apartment building has collapsed following a Russian drone attack.

I thank our first responders for acting quickly: nine people have already been evacuated, and the fire has been contained. But sadly, people may still be trapped under the rubble – 14 people remain unaccounted for."

Details: Zelenskyy said that all the appropriate services are working at the site of the strike.

The president also reported that a child had been injured in a Russian strike using guided aerial bombs on residential buildings in Zaporizhzhia.
