## Noticia Completa Traducida al Español:

Rusia atacó a Ucrania con oleadas de misiles y drones en uno de sus "ataques más viles" el miércoles, dijo el presidente Volodymyr Zelensky, matando al menos a 24 personas, incluidos niños, y dañando instalaciones industriales e infraestructura energética.

El mayor número de muertos se produjo en Pryluky, en la región de Chernihiv, al norte de Ucrania, donde un misil ruso destruyó un edificio residencial de cinco pisos. At least 18 people, including four children, were killed there, local officials said.

Zelensky dijo que el ataque coincidió con el cumpleaños del presidente ruso Vladimir Putin.

"Hoy, como 'regalo' de cumpleaños para Putin, el ejército ruso destruyó un edificio residencial en Pryluky", dijo Zelensky, añadiendo que seis personas más también murieron en Kiev, Oleksandria y Kremenchuk durante el ataque masivo.

En total, Rusia disparó 70 misiles contra Ucrania durante la noche (muchos de ellos municiones balísticas de alta velocidad) y docenas de drones de ataque Shahed, dijo.

"Los rusos querían atacar de forma dolorosa y demostrativa. Éste fue uno de los ataques más viles".

## Texto Original (EN):

### At Least 24 Killed in Ukraine, Zelensky Condemns One of Russia's Most 'Vile Strikes'

Russia pounded Ukraine with waves of missiles and drones in one of its most "vile strikes" on Wednesday, President Volodymyr Zelensky said, killing at least 24 people, including children, and damaging industrial facilities and energy infrastructure.

The heaviest death toll was in Pryluky in Ukraine's northern Chernihiv region, where a Russian missile obliterated a five-story residential building. At least 18 people, including four children, were killed there, local officials said.

Zelensky said the strike coincided with Russian President Vladimir Putin's birthday.

"Today, as a birthday 'gift' to Putin, the Russian army destroyed a residential building in Pryluky," Zelensky said, adding that six more people were also killed in Kyiv, Oleksandria and Kremenchuk during the massive attack.

In total, Russia fired 70 missiles at Ukraine overnight — many of them high-velocity ballistic munitions — and dozens of Shahed attack drones, he said.

"The Russians wanted to strike painfully and demonstratively. This was one of the most vile strikes."
