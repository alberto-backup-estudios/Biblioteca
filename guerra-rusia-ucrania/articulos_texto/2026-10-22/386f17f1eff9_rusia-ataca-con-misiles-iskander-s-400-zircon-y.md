## Texto Original (EN):

### Russia attacks with Iskander, S-400, Zircon and Oniks missiles and 210 drones: air defences down 181 UAVs

The Russians attacked Ukraine with Iskander-M, S-400, Zircon and Oniks missiles and 210 drones on the night of 21-22 October, The city of Kyiv has been the main target of the attack. Air defences have destroyed 181 UAVs.

Details: The attack began at 18:00 on 21 October. The Russians launched Iskander-M, S-400, Zircon and Oniks missiles from Russia's Kursk, Bryansk and Rostov oblasts.

They also deployed 210 Shahed (110 jet-powered), Gerbera and Italmas attack drones and Parodiya decoy UAVs.

The drones were launched from the Russian cities of Millerovo, Oryol, Kursk, Primorsko-Akhtarsk and Shatalovo as well as from temporarily occupied Donetsk and Hvardiiske (Crimea).

Kyiv has been the main target of the attack.

Quote: "As of 09:00, [Ukrainian] air defence forces had destroyed or jammed 181 Shahed (63 jet-powered), Gerbera and other types of drones. Another 10 attack drones remain airborne and combat operations are ongoing! Do not ignore air-raid warnings!"
