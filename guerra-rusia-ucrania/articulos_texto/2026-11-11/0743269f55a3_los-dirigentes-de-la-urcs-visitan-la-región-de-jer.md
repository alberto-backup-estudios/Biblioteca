## Noticia Completa Traducida al Español:

Los dirigentes de la Cruz Roja Ucraniana (URCS) visitaron la región de Jersón.

"En el cuarto aniversario de la liberación de Kherson, una delegación de la Cruz Roja Ucraniana encabezada por el presidente Maksym Dotsenko y el director general Illya Kletskovskyy visitó la región de Kherson. Como parte de la visita, se celebró una reunión de trabajo con representantes de las autoridades regionales y municipales, el Servicio Estatal de Emergencia de Ucrania y la rama regional de Kherson de la Cruz Roja Ucraniana", dijo la URCS en Facebook el miércoles.

### URCS leaders paying visit to Kherson region

The leadership of the Ukrainian Red Cross Society (URCS) has visited Kherson region.

“On the fourth anniversary of the liberation of Kherson, a Ukrainian Red Cross delegation led by President Maksym Dotsenko and Director General Illya Kletskovskyy visited Kherson Region. As part of the visit, a working meeting was held with representatives of the regional and city authorities, the State Emergency Service of Ukraine and the Kherson Regional Branch of the Ukrainian Red Cross,” URCS said on Facebook Wednesday.

URCS leadership visited the Kherson region

Participants discussed the current humanitarian and security situation in the region, winter preparedness, the needs of local communities and further coordination efforts to support the population.

Dotsenko and Kletskovsky also took part in the round table talk entitled "An inclusive approach to service delivery in emergency response."

During events marking the fourth anniversary of Kherson’s liberation, Maksym Dotsenko, President of the Ukrainian Red Cross, received the “For Dedication to Kherson Region” award.
