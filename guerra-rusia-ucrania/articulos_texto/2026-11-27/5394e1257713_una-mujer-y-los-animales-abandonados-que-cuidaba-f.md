Detalles: La misión de rescate se llevó a cabo al amparo de una unidad antidrones de las fuerzas del orden, y también se trajo a un especialista en comportamiento de perros policía para ayudar.

## Texto Original (EN):

### Woman and abandoned animals she cared for evacuated from Balabyne in Zaporizhzhia Oblast

Police officers and volunteers have evacuated Iryna, a local resident from Balabyne in Zaporizhzhia Oblast, along with the animals she had been caring for after their owners fled due to the war. Most of the animals have been placed in the care of animal welfare activists.

Details: The animals requiring help were looked after by local resident Iryna. She had sheltered numerous pets whose owners had left because of the hostilities. Iryna cared for the animals and refused to evacuate without them. However, due to intensifying attacks, Iryna decided to seek assistance with evacuation.

Quotes from Iryna: "Many abandoned animals gradually drifted over to me because I live here alone. There haven't really been many people around for a whole year. So [the animals] lived with me free-range, running about in the yard.

The situation is very dangerous right now. I asked the police and animal welfare organisations to help with the evacuation so that the animals could be taken to a safe place."

Details: The rescue mission took place under the cover of a law enforcement anti-drone unit, and a police dog behaviourist was also brought in to assist.
