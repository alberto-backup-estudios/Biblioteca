## Texto Original (EN):

### Two killed and two injured in Russian drone attacks on Kharkiv Oblast

A man and a woman have been killed in the Kupiansk district and two other women have been injured in the Lozova and Chuhuiv districts as the Russians attacked Kharkiv Oblast with drones on 17 November.

Source: Kharkiv Oblast Prosecutor's Office

Quote from the Prosecutor's Office: "The investigation reports state that on 17 November at around 02:00, a Russian UAV, preliminarily identified as a Geran-2 type, hit the village of Chervona Khvylia in the Kupiansk district. Two people were killed: a 58-year-old man and a 56-year-old woman. Two houses were destroyed and about six others were damaged."

Details: At around 08:05, a Russian drone, preliminarily identified as a Molniya type, hit an open area in the village of Lukashivka in the Blyzniuky

A 59-year-old woman was injured. She was taken to hospital with soft tissue bruises to her face and abdomen, as well as a laceration to her right thigh.

At approximately 11:25 in Slobozhanske, Chuhuiv district, a Russian jet-powered drone, preliminarily identified as a Geran-4 type, hit a grocery kiosk that was closed at the time. A fire broke out.
