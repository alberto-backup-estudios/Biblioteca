## Texto Original (EN):

### Twenty injured, including two children, in Russian attacks on Dnipropetrovsk Oblast

Twenty people, including two children, were injured in Russian attacks on the city of Dnipro and Dnipropetrovsk Oblast on 17 November.

Source: Oleksandr Hanzha, Head of Dnipropetrovsk Oblast Military Administration

Quote: "Twenty people have been injured. Two children are among them. The enemy attacked four districts more than 30 times, using drones, artillery and missiles.

In Dnipro, businesses and houses were damaged. Fourteen people were injured, including a seven-year-old boy. He is receiving outpatient treatment."

Details: In Pavlohrad, a business, houses and outbuildings were damaged. Two men aged 59 and 38 were taken to hospital in a moderate condition. A 61-year-old woman is receiving outpatient treatment.
