## Noticia Completa Traducida al Español:

El canciller alemán, Friedrich Merz, ha criticado duramente al ex canciller Gerhard Schröder por visitar al líder ruso Vladimir Putin para celebrar el cumpleaños de Putin.

Detalles: Steffen Meyer, primer portavoz adjunto del Gobierno federal, afirmó en Berlín que la canciller Merz está "profundamente indignada" por la reunión.

Merz dijo que "Putin sólo" es responsable de la guerra en Ucrania y sus órdenes causan "sufrimientos indescriptibles, víctimas civiles, familias destrozadas y muertes y heridos" todos los días.

En este contexto, la canciller considera "totalmente inmoral" que Schröder celebre con Putin.

Ni el Ministerio de Asuntos Exteriores, ni la Embajada de Alemania en Ucrania, ni el Ministerio Federal del Interior fueron informados con antelación de la visita, dijeron los ministerios pertinentes.

Schröder ya había sido criticado por varios políticos alemanes.

## Texto Original (EN):

### Merz criticises former chancellor Schröder's Russia visit to celebrate Putin's birthday as "totally immoral"

German Chancellor Friedrich Merz has sharply criticised former chancellor Gerhard Schröder for visiting Russian leader Vladimir Putin to celebrate Putin's birthday.

Details: Steffen Meyer, First Deputy Spokesperson for the Federal Government, said in Berlin that Chancellor Merz is "deeply outraged" by the meeting.

Merz said "Putin alone" is responsible for the war in Ukraine and his orders cause "unspeakable suffering, civilian casualties, families torn apart, and deaths and injuries" every day.

Against this backdrop, the chancellor considers it "totally immoral" for Schröder to be celebrating with Putin.

Neither the Foreign Office, nor the German Embassy in Ukraine, nor the Federal Ministry of the Interior were informed of the visit in advance, the relevant ministries said.

Schröder had already been criticised by a number of German politicians.
