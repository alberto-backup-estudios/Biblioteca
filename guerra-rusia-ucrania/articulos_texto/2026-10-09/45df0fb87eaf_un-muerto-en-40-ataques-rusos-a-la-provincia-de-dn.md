## Noticia Completa Traducida al Español:

Las fuerzas rusas lanzaron 40 ataques contra tres distritos del óblast de Dnipropetrovsk el 9 de octubre, utilizando drones, artillería y un misil. Una persona ha muerto en los ataques.

Fuente: Oleksandr Hanzha, Jefe de la Administración Militar del Óblast de Dnipropetrovsk; Servicio Estatal de Emergencia de Ucrania

Cita de Hanzha: "En el distrito de Synelnykove, las hromadas Zaitseve y Petropavlivka
han sido afectados. Una empresa agrícola e instalaciones de infraestructura resultaron dañadas. Se produjeron incendios. Una persona murió".

En el distrito de Nikopol, las tropas rusas atacaron la ciudad de Nikopol y las hromadas de Pokrovske, Marhanets, Myrove, Mozolevske y Chervonohryhorivka. Un bloque de apartamentos, casas, empresas y una infraestructura resultaron dañados. Se produjeron incendios.

## Texto Original (EN):

### One killed in 40 Russian attacks on Dnipropetrovsk Oblast – photos

Russian forces launched 40 attacks on three districts in Dnipropetrovsk Oblast on 9 October, using drones, artillery and a missile. One person has been killed in the strikes.

Source: Oleksandr Hanzha, Head of Dnipropetrovsk Oblast Military Administration; State Emergency Service of Ukraine

Quote from Hanzha: "In the Synelnykove district, the Zaitseve and Petropavlivka  hromadas
have been affected. An agricultural business and infrastructure facilities were damaged. Fires broke out. One person was killed."

Details: In Kryvyi Rih, the Russians damaged infrastructure facilities. In the Zelenodolsk hromada, houses were damaged.

In the Nikopol district, Russian troops attacked the city of Nikopol and the Pokrovske, Marhanets, Myrove, Mozolevske and Chervonohryhorivka hromadas. An apartment block, houses, companies and an infrastructure facility were damaged. Fires broke out.

Background: On 8 October, two 62-year-old men were killed in the Vasylkivka hromada in Dnipropetrovsk Oblast as a result of a Russian guided aerial bomb strike.
