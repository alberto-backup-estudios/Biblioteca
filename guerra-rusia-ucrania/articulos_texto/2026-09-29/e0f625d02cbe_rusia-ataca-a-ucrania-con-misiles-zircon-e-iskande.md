## Texto Original (EN):

### Russia attacks Ukraine with Zircon and Iskander missiles and 152 drones: air defence downs 109 aerial assets

Russian forces attacked Ukraine with a Zircon/Oniks anti-ship missile, two Iskander-M/S-400 ballistic missiles and 152 drones on the night of 28-29 September. Air defence forces have downed 109 aerial assets.

Details: The Russians started the attack at 18:00 on 28 September. They launched a Zircon/Oniks anti-ship missile and two Iskander-M/S-400 ballistic missiles from Kursk Oblast.

They also used 152 Shahed (82 jet-powered) and Gerbera attack drones and Parodiya decoy UAVs.

The drones were launched from the Russian cities of Oryol, Millerovo, Kursk and Primorsko-Akhtarsk as well as the temporarily occupied territory of Donetsk and from Hvardiiske and Chauda in temporarily occupied Crimea.

Aircraft, anti-aircraft missile units, electronic warfare assets, unmanned systems units and mobile fire groups from Ukraine's defence forces have been involved in repelling the attack.
