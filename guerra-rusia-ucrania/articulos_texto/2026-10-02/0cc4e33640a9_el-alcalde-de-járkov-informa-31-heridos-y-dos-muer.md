## Noticia Completa Traducida al Español:

El número de víctimas en Járkov sigue aumentando; A las 17:20 se sabe que entre ellos hay tres niños, dijo el alcalde Ihor Terekhov.

"El número de víctimas ha aumentado a 33, incluidos dos muertos y tres niños", dijo Terekhov en su canal Telegram.

Informes anteriores indicaron que dos personas murieron y otras 28 resultaron heridas como resultado de los ataques de tres misiles de crucero rusos en los distritos de Slobidsky y Nemyshliansky de Kharkiv.

## Texto Original (EN):

### Kharkiv mayor reports 31 injured, two killed

The number of casualties in Kharkiv continues to rise; as of 17:20, it is known that three children are among them, Mayor Ihor Terekhov said.

"The number of casualties has risen to 33 – including two fatalities and three children," Terekhov said on his Telegram channel.

Earlier reports indicated that two people were killed and 28 others were injured as a result of strikes by three Russian cruise missiles in Slobidsky and Nemyshliansky districts of Kharkiv.
