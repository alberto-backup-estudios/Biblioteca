## Noticia Completa Traducida al Español:

El presidente ucraniano, Volodymyr Zelenskyy, anunció el primer uso exitoso en combate de balística táctica de producción nacional.

## Texto Original (EN):

### Ukraine used its domestically produced FP-7 tactical ballistic missile in combat for first time – Zelenskyy

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy announced the first successful combat use of domestically produced tactical ballistics.

"Ukrainian tactical ballistics. FP-7. First combat use. Thank you for the result," the president said on Telegram on Thursday.
